Finding your way around

Four things to recognise. Once these make sense the rest is just typing.

  • A Tabs across the topHome, Leads, Accounts, Contacts, Opportunities… Mark done›

    Each tab is a type of record: Accounts are companies, Contacts are people, Opportunities are deals, Leads are people you have not qualified yet. Click a tab for a list; click a row to open the record.

  • B List viewsYour filtered tables Mark done›

    The dropdown next to a list’s name switches between saved filters — My Opportunities, Closing This Month, Recently Viewed. Changing the view never changes data.

  • C Shared accountsSome of your customers are also DXR customers Mark done›

    Where a company already existed in Salesforce, your record was joined to it. The account owner may be a DXR colleague; you are on the Account Team panel and your deals, contacts and history are all there. This is the group seeing one customer once.

  • D Saving is not the same as advancingThe single most useful thing to know Mark done›

    Editing a field and saving is always fine. Changing the Stage is what triggers the checks. Your migrated deals sit at the stage they had in HubSpot; the checks only apply the first time you move one forward.

    On the stage path at the top of the deal, choose Closed, then Closed Won. That opens the work package form. Editing the Stage field directly will be refused.

  • 2Fill in the work packageWhat Projects and Finance needMark done›

    The form asks for the contract details and a short outline of the work. The Order Form No. is filled in from your latest order form.

  • 3SubmitSalesforce does the hand-overMark done›

    If something was wrong, use Resend Work Package on the closed deal.

Your first week. Six steps, per deal; ticks are remembered on this browser.

  • 1Open My OpportunitiesEvery open CyberCrowd deal you ownMark done›

    Click Opportunities, then switch the list to My Opportunities. Click the Close Date column to sort oldest first.

    Sales app›Opportunities›list: My Opportunities›sort by Close Date
  • 2Close what is deadOld HubSpot deals nobody is workingMark done›

    Your HubSpot pipeline came across as it was — including deals that stopped moving years ago. 474 open CyberCrowd deals have a close date before 2026. If it is dead, move it to Closed Lost with a Loss Reason and a line of Loss Detail. Losing a deal needs no MEDDPICC evidence.

  • 3Fix the close date on what is aliveYour honest dateMark done›

    If the deal is real, give it the date you actually expect a decision. A past date on an open deal shows as overdue on your manager’s report.

  • 4Check Service Line, set Funding RouteTwo fields HubSpot never hadMark done›

    You cannot leave Qualify without either. HubSpot never had a service line, so we filled it in from the deal name — check it is right (56 deals with no clue in the name say Other). Funding Route is yours to set. Open the deal, go to Details, click the pencil, save.

    The deal name updates itself when you change the service line, type or close date.

    A deal over £0 and under £5k needs nothing more — it skips the MEDDPICC grades.

  • 5Give it a Next Step with a future dateThe rule every stage move checksMark done›

    Write the next action in Next Step and a date of today or later in Next Step Date. Without it, no stage will move.

  • 6Move the stage when you can evidence itRead "To Advance, Still Needed"Mark done›

    The field To Advance, Still Needed on every deal lists exactly what is missing for the next stage. Fill those, then move the stage on the path at the top.

When it will not save

The messages you are most likely to hit, and what each means.

Cannot leave Qualify yet. The field 'To Advance, Still Needed'…

Open the deal and read To Advance, Still Needed — it lists exactly which fields or grades are missing. On a migrated deal the usual culprit is Funding Route.

Before you can advance, this deal needs a Next Step and a Next Step Date…

Next Step is blank, or its date has passed. Most migrated deals are in this state. Write the next action and give it a date of today or later, then move the stage.

Cannot leave Solution Validation yet…

Same idea: To Advance, Still Needed lists what is missing. Champion must be Confirmed Advocacy or higher, and Procurement Route must be set.

Competitive tender of £10k+: set "Bid Decision"…

Before a tender of £10k or more goes to Proposal, record the bid/no-bid call: Bid Decision (Bid or Bid - Conditional), Bid Decision Date and Bid Decision By.

Cannot set Closed Won. Set: "Contract / PO Number"…

Fill Contract / PO Number, Signed Date, Contract Start Date, Win Reason, and either Contract End Date or tick One-Time Deal.

Close this deal from the stage path at the top of the deal (Closed, then Closed Won)…

Won deals close through the work package form. On the path at the top choose Closed, then Closed Won, and fill in the form.

Cannot set Closed Lost. Needs Loss Reason and Loss Detail…

Fill Loss Reason and Loss Detail (a sentence on why). If the reason is Lost to Competitor, also set Competitor Won - and if that is Other, type the competitor in Winning Supplier (Other). Easiest from the stage path: Closed, then Closed Lost, which shows all four fields.

A Closed Lost opportunity cannot be reopened.

Use the Create Re-Tender action on the deal instead. It makes a new linked deal and keeps the original loss.

Set "Framework Name" when "Procurement Route" is…

Framework Call-off and Mini-Competition need the framework named (e.g. G-Cloud 14).

A general rule

The message appears in a red box at the top of the form, often scrolled out of sight. If a save seems to do nothing, scroll up. It is a checklist, not a telling-off.
